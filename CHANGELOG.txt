CoA Inspect Tree v0.20

Visual refresh
- Rebuilt the main frame with a dark Character Advancement theme, framed border, title bar, and accent line.
- Redesigned talent nodes with depth, shadows, gloss, learned-node glow, clearer rank plates, and stronger inactive states.
- Improved tab headers and separators for easier scanning.
- Improved Compare and spec selector styling.

Functionality
- Panel now automatically opens on the left when there is not enough room on the right side of the Inspect window.
- Added mouse-wheel vertical scrolling with a visible scroll indicator.
- Added a close button that remains respected until fresh inspect data is rendered.
- Added /cit show, /cit hide, and /cit reset commands.
- Improved support for numeric icons, full texture paths, and standard icon names.
- Improved tooltip fallback text and learned/unlearned information.

Compatibility
- Existing CoA tree reading, build comparison, rank detection, choice-node collapsing, and scale persistence remain intact.
- Designed for the same 3.3.5 / CoA environment as v0.10.
